The other side

Tech isn’t the only language I speak. Here’s what I do outside of IT.

Music

Piano, guitar and percussion. I taught music at high school level for over three years and coached students through ABRSM exams. I don’t just play, I teach people how to hear.

Production

Building tracks from scratch: arrangement, mixing, sound design. It taught me patience, attention to detail and creative problem solving.

Video editing

Cuts, colour and pacing. If there’s a story in the footage, I’ll find it. It’s another system to understand and master.

Basketball & gaming

Basketball taught me to read a room fast and adapt. Gaming sharpened my pattern recognition and strategic thinking.

Same brain, different weights

The brain that hears a chord progression and knows why it resolves is the one that looks at a system error and traces it back to the root cause. Creativity and technical thinking aren’t opposites. They’re the same muscle.
