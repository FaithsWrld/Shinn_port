About

I’m Faith Kola-Olusa, but most people know me as Shinn (信). I’m Nigerian-British, born in Abuja and now working in the UK. I teach myself by building things, and my sites usually start with a mood before they start with a wireframe.

By day I work in production. By night I’m studying for a move into IT support and cybersecurity, taking freelance web design work, and learning Roblox game development. I also own a large Nigerian Minecraft community, which is where I learned most of what I know about running systems for real people.

I also like to question things. History and philosophy are long-running interests of mine, and I like to check where a claim comes from before I believe it.
